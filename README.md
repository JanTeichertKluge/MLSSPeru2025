# MLSS 2025 – Machine Learning Summer School (Arequipa, Peru)

Welcome! This repository contains the lecture materials, code notebooks, and references used during the **Machine Learning Summer School (MLSS 2025)** held at **Universidad Católica San Pablo**, Arequipa, Peru.

---

## 📅 Event Overview

- **Dates:** August 2–13, 2025  
- **Location:** Universidad Católica San Pablo, Arequipa, Peru  
- **Website:** [eventosdiee.ucsp.edu.pe/mlss](https://eventosdiee.ucsp.edu.pe/mlss/)  

---

## Topics of Lectures

* Yarin Gal - Uncertainty in LLMs, Bayesian Methods
* Mauricio Álvarez - Gaussian Processes, Bayesian Optimization
* Bianca Dumitrascu - Probabilistic modelling of cell types, Nonlinearity and multi-modal representations, Dealing with Space and, if time, with Time
* Yuge Shi - Diffusion Models
* Omar U. Florez - Efficient Pretraining of Large Language Models (LLMs)
* Himanshu Sahni - Diffusion Language Models
* Finale Doshi-Velez - Intro Reinforcement Learning, Explainable AI
* Victoria Dean - Fairness, AI in Society
* David Martínez-Rubio - Optimization
* Tal Linzen - Language Models
* Grigoris Chrysos - Robustness in Deep learning, Adversarial Attacks, Towards trustworthy foundation models with privacy
* Abhishek Gupta - Imitation and Offline Reinforcement Learning, Inverse Reinforcement Learning
* Francesco Locatello - Causality, Causal Representation Learning
* Dhruv Shah - Robot Learning
* Jindong Gu - Responsible Generative AI: Ensuring Safety in Textual and Visual Generation
